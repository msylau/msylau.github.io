**We are looking for passionate new PhD students, Postdocs, and Master students to join the team!**


The **Lau Lab** harnesses the power of statistics and machine learning to uncover the dynamics of infectious disease spread in space and time. We build innovative models and novel algorithms that blend **statistical inference**, **computational models**, and **ML/AI techniques** to inform outbreak response, guide public health decisions, and drive scientific discovery. *Our work lives at the crossroads of **statistics**, **computer science**, **applied mathematics**, **epidemiology/ecology** and **evolutionary biology***. From phylodynamic models that jointly characterize viral transmission and evolution to interpretable deep learning tools for real-time spatiotemporal forecasting, we tackle complex challenges in epidemic modeling. Based in the Department of Biostatistics and Bioinformatics at Emory University, our lab thrives on interdisciplinary collaboration and a shared mission to transform data into actionable insight for public health.
